Daily consumption

How much alkalinity, calcium and magnesium the tank actually uses each day. It adds back what you dose and what water changes add or remove, and uses a trend line through your tests, so one off test doesn't swing it.

When to test for an accurate number

Same time every day. Alkalinity is used faster while the lights are on, so tests taken at different times of day look like changes that aren't real. Pick one time (first thing in the morning before the lights come on is easy to keep) and stick to it. If your doser runs several times a day, test at the same point between doses, not right after one.

Right before a water change, not after. A test just before the change shows everything the tank used since the last one, and tells you how far off target the change needs to correct. A test right after shows the new water mixed in, not the tank.

Hold doses steady while you measure. Change the dose only after the check, then test alkalinity daily for a few days to confirm the new dose holds.

More tests beat better tests. Home kits vary by a few tenths of a dKH from test to test. A trend line through a week or two of daily tests averages that out; two tests can't.

Repeat the check when you add corals, change lights or photoperiod, or once a month while the tank is growing in; consumption rises as corals grow.

Privacy

This copy runs inside Claude, and its log is stored with this Claude artifact under your account. The notes below describe the standalone Holdfast website.

On this device. Your log, photos and settings are saved in this browser's storage on this device. Nothing leaves the device unless you sign in to sync.

If you sign in to sync. Your records and photos are copied to the Holdfast database (hosted by Supabase) so your other devices can load them. The database only lets each account read and change its own records. The app's owner runs that database and can technically reach it; it is used only to run sync, is never sold or shared, and isn't looked at except to fix a problem you report.

No ads, analytics or tracking cookies. Loading the app does contact a few services that see your connection (like any website): Cloudflare hosts the app, jsDelivr serves code libraries, Google Fonts serves the typefaces, and Supabase handles sign-in and sync if you use them.

Your data stays yours. Download a full backup any time under Data & backup. To have your synced account and everything in it deleted, ask through the feedback link.

About the numbers

Garbage in, garbage out. Every suggestion here comes from what you log. Test on a schedule, log every test, water change and dosing change, and calibrate your test gear. Old, missing or wrong numbers give wrong advice, and no app replaces looking at your tank every day.

Dosing, consumption and water change figures are estimates built from product labels (or strengths worked out from the maker's stated concentration) and your own test results. Check them against the product label, change doses gradually, and retest before and after any big change. Light and equipment details are starting points; your fixture's app and manual have the final word.

Product, brand and fixture names (Noopsyche, K7, Brightwell, Red Sea, Tropic Marin, Seachem, ESV, Aquaforest, Bulk Reef Supply, AI, EcoTech, Kessil, NICREW, Hanna and others; AI means Aqua Illumination) are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the products the app works with.
