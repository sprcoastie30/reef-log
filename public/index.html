Power, beam and mounting height are from Noopsyche's K7 V4 product page; Noopsyche's manual advises starting at 50% total brightness when moving up from weaker lights, and the planner flags any channel above that. Noopsyche doesn't publish channel names or the QR layout. The channel names, their order, and the code layout here come from the open-source K7 LED Controller project, which documents the K7 Pro as White, Royal Blue, Green, UV, Cyan, Red. That project says "K7 Pro" without naming a generation, so check the order once on your Pro IV with the channel test.

Your tank profile lists a different light. The planner, QR codes and controller presets here are built for Noopsyche K7 Pro fixtures; the PAR log works with any light.

Noopsyche app (QR code)

Makes a QR code for the Noopsyche app's Import. The order below is the K7 Pro order documented by the K7 LED Controller project. Because it hasn't been confirmed on a Pro IV, the button unlocks once you've run the channel test and ticked the box. The code also carries manual-mode values; the planner sets those to your program's brightest hour.

K7 LED Controller (ESP32 board, PC Bridge or Android app)

For the open-source controller that drives the light over its own Wi-Fi. Web pages can't send directly to devices on your home network, so programs move by file: download a preset here, then open the controller and use Import Preset, review it, and press Push. Presets use the controller's own K7 Pro channel order, so they don't depend on the QR test.
